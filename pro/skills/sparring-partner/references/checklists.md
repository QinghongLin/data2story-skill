# Dimension Checklists

For the Pressure-test sweep (Phase 4). Walk the relevant list and explicitly name what has **not** been discussed yet — the gaps are the point. If the idea's domain isn't listed, build a checklist from first principles by asking: "what categories of consideration does this kind of artifact have?"

## Software / system design
Problem & users · Data model · Interfaces / API · State & lifecycle · Failure modes & error handling · Concurrency · Scale & performance · Security & authorization · Privacy & data handling · Observability (logs / metrics / traces) · Testing strategy · Deployment & rollback · Migration / backfill · Cost · Maintenance & ownership · Dependencies (and failure of each) · Edge cases · UX & accessibility · Documentation.

## Product / startup idea
Problem & who has it · Target user & segment · Value proposition · Alternatives & competition (including "do nothing") · Market size · Business model & pricing · Distribution / go-to-market · Moat / defensibility · Unit economics · Key risks & assumptions · MVP scope · Success metrics · Regulatory / legal.

## Research project (academic)
Research question · Novelty vs. prior art · Hypothesis · Methodology · Data & sources · Controls & confounds · Analysis plan · Internal validity · External validity / generalization · Reproducibility · Ethics & approvals · Feasibility (time / equipment / skills) · Expected results & interpretation · Failure modes · Contribution & significance.

## Writing / argument
Thesis · Audience & what they already believe · Structure & flow · Evidence per claim · Strongest counterarguments addressed · Narrative arc · Hook · Clarity & jargon · Scope (what's deliberately left out) · The one takeaway the reader keeps.

## General decision
Options (including the status quo) · Decision criteria · Reversibility (one-way vs. two-way door) · Time horizon · Stakeholders affected · Risks & worst case · Second-order effects · Base rate for this kind of decision · What would change the decision · Cost of delay.
