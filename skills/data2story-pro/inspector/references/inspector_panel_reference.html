What the numbers say

Across the five rail lines, weekday boardings rose 8.5% system-wide between 2023 and 2024, but the gains are lopsided: the Gold line jumped 22.4% while the Green line actually slipped 4.2%. Click to see the per-line table, the code, and re-run the arithmetic in your browser.

Treating daily boardings as a noisy draw around the mean, a Monte-Carlo estimate puts the chance that any given weekday tips over the 245k capacity-alert line at roughly 7.4%, or about 19 alert days a year. Re-running it in the browser uses a reduced sample, so it lands within Monte-Carlo noise of that figure.

The live operator feed reports the latest hourly boardings per line. That cell pulls data over the network, so it cannot run in the browser sandbox — the source card shows the read-only code and points to the frozen snapshot and the notebook cell instead.

Why it matters
