# Question Bank

High-leverage questions by type. Choose the **one or two that bite hardest right now** — never fire a list at the user. The best question is usually the one that surfaces an unexamined assumption or reframes the problem.

## Clarifying / definitional
- When you say "[term]", what exactly do you mean — and what does it exclude?
- Can you give a concrete example? Now an edge case?
- What does "done" / "success" look like, specifically?

## Assumption-surfacing
- What has to be true for this to work?
- Which of those assumptions would hurt most if it's wrong?
- What are you treating as fixed that might actually be changeable?
- Whose problem is this — and did they say so, or are we guessing?

## Evidence / justification
- How do you know that? What's the evidence vs. the hope?
- What would you expect to see if this were false?
- Has anyone tried this? What happened to them?

## Alternative / counterfactual
- What are two other ways to get the same outcome?
- If you *couldn't* do it this way, what would you do?
- What would a skeptic / a competitor / a beginner do instead?

## Consequence / second-order
- If this works, what does it break or change downstream?
- And then what? (repeat)
- Who is worse off if this succeeds?

## Reframe / meta
- Is this the real problem, or a symptom of a deeper one?
- What problem, if solved instead, would make this one irrelevant?
- Are we optimizing the right thing?

## Goal / success criteria
- What's the actual goal *behind* this goal?
- How will you measure whether it worked?
- What would make you abandon it?

## Constraint / resource
- What's the hard limit — time, money, people, attention?
- What's the smallest version that still tests the core idea?

## Risk / failure
- What's the most likely way this dies?
- What's the worst case — and can you survive it?
- What would an adversary exploit?

## Prioritization / trade-off
- If you could keep only one feature/aspect, which?
- What are you willing to be bad at?

## Falsification
- What evidence would change your mind?
- What's the cheapest experiment that could kill this idea fast?
